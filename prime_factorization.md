### Prime Factorization

**Key Concepts:**
- **Prime factorization** involves decomposing a number into its **prime factors**.
- **Prime factor** is a **prime number** that divides a whole number without leaving a remainder.
- **Prime number** is a **whole number** greater than $1$ that is divisible only by $1$ and itself.

**Prime Factorization Process:**
- Divide the number by the **smallest possible prime factor**;
- Repeat this process until the result is equals $1$;
- Express the original number as a product of prime factors with their respective powers.

**Example:**
Decompose $12600$ into its prime factors.

- smallest possible prime factor = $2$
  - $12600 \div 2 = 6300$
  - $6300 \div 2 = 3150$
  - $3150 \div 2 = 1575$

- smallest possible prime factor = $3$
  - $1575 \div 3 = 525$
  - $525 \div 3 = 175$

- smallest possible prime factor = $5$
  - $175 \div 5 = 35$
  - $35 \div 5 = 7$

- smallest possible prime factor = $7$
  - $7 \div 7 = \boxed{1}$ (factorization process completed)

Therefore, the prime factorization of $12600$ is: $\boxed{2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5^2 \times 7^1}$
